# Khung Dao Tao The First Cloud Journey (FCJ)

- Danh muc: Training / Framework
- Ngay tao: 2026-09-18

## 1. Dinh nghia va muc dich
Khung chuong trinh dao tao chuyen sau ve Cloud va AWS do cong dong AWS Study Group Vietnam xay dung, nham ho tro cac ky su va thuc tap sinh tiep can tu kien thuc co ban den nang luc thiet ke kien truc dam may chuan Well-Architected.

## 2. Dac diem ky thuat chinh
- Cau truc 7 module ky thuat lon.
- Hon 100 workshop thuc hanh co huong dan chi tiet tung buoc.
- Lo trinh thuc tap 12 tuan chia thanh 3 giai doan ro rang tu nen tang den do an tot nghiep.

## 3. Cach ap dung trong du an
Cung cap danh muc huong dan thuc hanh, he thong kiem tra tien do va cac tieu chuan tot nghiep duoc luu tru trong `raw/labs/` va `docs/`.
