# Khai Niem: Cau Truc 7 Phan Cua Website Bao Cao Thuc Tap

- Thuat ngu: FCAJ 7-Part Workshop Structure
- Thuoc chu de: [[fcj-training-framework]]
- Ngay tao: 2026-09-18

---

## 1. Dinh nghia va y nghia
Day la bo khung noi dung bat buoc phai co trong Website Bao cao thuc tap (Internship Report) cua hoc vien FCAJ Bootcamp, duoc xay dung dua tren mau thuc te cua cac hoc vien xuat sac khoa truoc.

## 2. Chi tiet 7 phan noi dung
1. `1-worklog`: Nhat ky cong viec hang tuan tu Tuan 1 den Tuan 12. Ghi ro nhiem vu, ket qua dat duoc va kho khan tung tuan.
2. `2-proposal`: Ban de xuat du an Capstone. Bao gom: Executive Summary, Problem Statement, Solution, Architecture Diagram, Technology Stack, High-Level Flow, Prerequisites, Roadmap va Expected Outcomes.
3. `3-blogsposted`: Danh sach 3 bai blog chuyen mon da dang tren cac nen tang (Medium, Viblo, Dev.to, LinkedIn) kem noi dung tom tat va link bai viet.
4. `4-eventparticipated`: Toi thieu 3 su kien cong dong FCAJ / AWS da tham gia. Ghi ro thoi gian, dia diem, vai tro, noi dung hoc hoi va bai hoc rut ra.
5. `5-workshop`: Huong dan thuc hanh du an ky thuat chinh theo dang step-by-step:
   - Overview & Prerequisites
   - Thiet lap Network / VPC
   - Cau hinh Application Services (Database, Storage, Secret)
   - Trien khai Compute (Lambda / ECS)
   - Cau hinh Domain / HTTPS / CDN
   - Thiet lap CI/CD & Monitoring (CloudWatch)
   - Testing & Clean-up (Xoa tai nguyen)
6. `6-self-evaluation`: Tu danh gia 8 tieu chi: Kien thuc, Kha nang hoc hoi, Tinh chu dong, Ky luat, Giao tiep, Teamwork, Giai quyet van de, Dong gop.
7. `7-feedback`: Cam nhan, muc do hai long, gop y cai thien cho ban to chuc chuong trinh.
