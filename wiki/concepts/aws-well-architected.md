## 1. Dinh nghia va y nghia
Khung kien truc AWS Well-Architected giup cac kien truc su dam may xay dung ha tang an toan, hieu nang cao, co tinh phuc hoi va hieu qua chi phi cho cac ung dung cua minh.

## 2. Nam tru cot cot loi
1. Operational Excellence (Xuat sac van hanh): Chay va giam sat he thong de mang lai gia tri kinh doanh va lien tuc cai tien quy trinh.
2. Security (Bao mat): Bao ve du lieu, he thong va tai nguyen thong qua danh gia rui ro va chien luoc giam thieu.
3. Reliability (Do tin cay): Kha nang he thong phuc hoi sau su co, tu dong cap phat tai nguyen va dap ung nhu cau.
4. Performance Efficiency (Hieu qua hieu nang): Su dung tai nguyen dien toan hieu qua de dap ung cac yeu cau he thong.
5. Cost Optimization (Toi uu chi phi): Tranh lang phi chi phi va toi uu hoa hoa don dien toan dam may.
