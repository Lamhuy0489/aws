# Khai Niem: Tieu Chi Danh Gia Thuc Tap FCAJ (Evaluation Rubric)

- Thuat ngu: FCAJ Internship Evaluation Rubric
- Thuoc chu de: [[fcj-training-framework]]
- Ngay tao: 2026-09-18

---

## 1. Dinh nghia va y nghia
Thang diem danh gia thuc tap FCAJ la bo tieu chuan 10 diem dung de xet duyet cap chung nhan hoan thanh khoa hoc va xac nhan moc thuc tap cho sinh vien. Diem tong ket can dat toi thieu 7.0 / 10.

## 2. Co cau diem tong the
- Diem Chuyen can (1.0 diem): Tinh dua tren 10 buoi co mat tai van phong (0.1 diem / buoi).
- Diem Thai do (1.0 diem): Tinh chu dong, tu giac hoc tap, hop tac voi dong doi va mentor.
- Diem Tac phong (1.0 diem): Tuan thu quy dinh ve trang phuc dong phuc FCAJ, quan dai, gio giac va quy dinh van phong.
- Diem Tham du Event (1.0 diem): Tham gia toi thieu 3 su kien cong dong (Community Day, Workshop, Meetup).
- Diem Du an Project (6.0 diem): Chat luong cua bao cao thuc tap va workshop ky thuat.

## 3. Phan tich chi tiet diem Project (6.0 diem)
- Y tuong va muc tieu (1.0 diem): Bai toan thuc te, mo ta ro nguoi dung va ket qua mong doi.
- Kien truc va thiet ke ky thuat (2.0 diem): Diagram dep, su dung toi thieu 3 dich vu AWS, co kien truc bao mat va scale.
- Trien khai step-by-step (2.0 diem): Huong dan chi tiet co screenshot, CLI, test va muc clean-up xoa tai nguyen.
- Trinh bay va song ngu (0.5 diem): Day du ca tieng Anh va tieng Viet, khong loi chinh ta, format chuan Hugo.
- Dong gop ca nhan (0.5 diem): Co tinh sang tao rieng, khong sao chep nguyen ban, neu ro kho khan va bai hoc kinh nghiem.
