## 1. Tom tat noi dung (200 - 400 tu)
Tai lieu xac dinh lo trinh thuc tap gom 3 giai doan chinh voi muc tieu chuyen hoa kien thuc ly thuyet thanh ky nang kien truc thuc te tren AWS:
- Giai doan 1 (Tuan 1 - 4): Nen tang Cloud & Thuc hanh Co ban. Hoan thanh 15 bai lab cot loi, nam vung VPC, IAM, EC2, S3 va cach thiet lap hanh lang chi phi.
- Giai doan 2 (Tuan 5 - 8): Chuyen sau Serverless, Container va Toi uu kien truc. Trien khai Docker tren ECS Fargate, viet kich ban ha tang Terraform, ap dung AWS Well-Architected.
- Giai doan 3 (Tuan 9 - 12): Thuc hien Do an Tot nghiep Agentic RAG va Bao cao. Xay dung he thong hoi dap thong minh tren kien truc Serverless, danh gia do chinh xac va toi uu chi phi.

Cung cap cac tieu chi danh gia ro rang ve tien do, chat luong ma nguon, viet tai lieu ky thuat va bao cao ket qua.
