## 1. Tom tat noi dung (200 - 400 tu)
Tai lieu tong hop toan bo khung dao tao va danh muc hon 100 workshop thuc hanh cua chuong trinh The First Cloud Journey (FCJ) do cong dong AWS Study Group Vietnam bien soan. Noi dung bao trum 7 module ky thuat lon tu co ban den nang cao:
1. Kham pha dich vu AWS: Nen tang compute, storage, networking, database (VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Route 53, CloudWatch).
2. Dich chuyen len AWS: Quy trinh chuyen doi may chu va CSDL (VM Import, DMS, SCT, DRS).
3. Toi uu he thong: 5 tru cot Well-Architected Framework (Van hanh, Bao mat, Do tin cay, Hieu nang, Chi phi).
4. Quan tri va Tuan thu: IAM Identity Center, AWS Organizations, Control Tower, Config, CloudTrail.
5. Trien khai ung dung hien dai: Container hoa voi ECS, EKS va kien truc Serverless (Lambda, API Gateway, Step Functions).
6. Tri tue nhan tao tao sinh (GenAI): AWS Bedrock, Prompt Engineering, Agentic RAG, Kendra, PartyRock.
7. Xay dung kien truc giai phap: Cac bai toan tich hop thuc te cho thuong mai dien tu, streaming, va he thong thanh toan.
