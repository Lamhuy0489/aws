## 1. Noi dung chinh
Tai lieu quy dinh chi tiet ve nghia vu va quyen loi cua sinh vien tham gia chuong trinh thuc tap FCAJ Workforce Bootcamp 2026. Cac yeu cau cot loi bao gom viec len van phong tai Ha Noi (Tang 7, Toa nha Grand Terra, 36 Cat Linh) toi thieu 10 buoi / 3 thang, tham gia toi thieu 3 su kien cong dong va hoan thanh bao cao thuc tap dang website workshop song ngu.

Diem tong ket chuong trinh tinh tren thang diem 10: Chuyen can (1.0), Thai do (1.0), Tac phong (1.0), Tham du Event (1.0), va Du an Project (6.0). Sinh vien phai dat toi thieu 7.0 / 10 diem de duoc cong nhan hoan thanh va nhan moc thuc tap.

## 2. Thong tin can chu y
- Project chiem 60% tong so diem, phai duoc viet bang 2 ngon ngu (tieng Anh va tieng Viet).
- Bat buoc deploy bao cao duoi dang website Hugo len GitHub Pages.
- Phai dam bao cac yeu cau ve an toan bao mat (IAM Least Privilege, khong hard-code access key) va phan clean-up tai nguyen de khong bi tru diem.
