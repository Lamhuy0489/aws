# Noi Quy Thuc Tap FCAJ Workforce Bootcamp 2026

Nguon: https://hn-rules.awsfcaj.com
To chuc: First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) / AWS Study Group

---

## 1. Tong quan chuong trinh
FCAJ Workforce Bootcamp 2026 la chuong trinh dao tao cong nghe chuyen sau danh cho sinh vien Viet Nam, duoc tai tro boi AWS. Chuong trinh van hanh boi cong dong FCAJ voi hon 50.000 thanh vien, lien ket cung hon 30 truong dai hoc tren toan quoc.

## 2. Quy dinh ve chuyen can va diem danh
- Dieu kien nhan moc thuc tap:
  - Phai len van phong toi thieu 10 ngay full time trong vong 3 thang.
  - Tan suat toi thieu: 1 lan / tuan.
  - Tham gia toi thieu 3 buoi Event cua cong dong FCAJ / AWS.
  - Hoan thanh Project bao cao theo dung quy dinh.
- Diem danh dua tren he thong dang ky va check-in tai van phong.

## 3. Quy dinh ve thoi gian, tac phong va ra vao
- Thoi gian lam viec tai van phong: 09:00 den 12:00 tu thu 7.
- Dia diem lam viec: Tang 7, Toa nha Grand Terra, so 36 Cat Linh, phuong Dong Da, TP. Ha Noi.
- Trang phuc:
  - Bat buoc mac ao dong phuc FCAJ khi den van phong (neu da co).
  - Bat buoc mac quan dai, khong mac quan short, khong di dep le.
- Ra vao van phong:
  - Di qua cua chinh, check-in voi bao ve tai le tan.
  - Chi hoat dong tai khu vuc duoc phan cong.
- An uong va do dung:
  - Khong an uong tai khu vuc lam viec chinh.
  - Khong su dung nuoc ngot, bia, ruou hoac do an co san cua van phong khi chua duoc phep.

## 4. Quy dinh bat buoc ve Project (Bao cao cuoi khoa)
- Ngon ngu: Bat buoc phai viet bang 2 ngon ngu (Song ngu: Tieng Anh va Tieng Viet).
- Hinh thuc trinh bay: Khuyen khich va yeu cau tao Website Workshop dung Hugo dua tren Workshop-template cua FCAJ va deploy len GitHub Pages.
- Cac phan bat buoc trong bao cao:
  1. Thong tin sinh vien: Ho ten, SDT, Email, Truong, Chuyen nganh, Cong ty thuc tap, Vi tri thuc tap, Thoi gian.
  2. Worklog (Nhat ky cong viec theo tuan): Ghi ro tu Week 1 den Week 12 (Cong viec thuc hien, ket qua dat duoc).
  3. Proposal (De xuat du an): Tong quan, muc tieu, bai toan giai quyet, kien truc giai phap, timeline, ngan sach, rui ro.
  4. Events Participated: Toi thieu 3 event (Ten event, thoi gian, dia diem, vai tro, noi dung chinh, bai hoc rut ra).
  5. Workshop (Du an ky thuat chinh): Overview, Prerequisite, So do kien truc, Lab tung buoc (step-by-step), Testing/Validation, Clean-up tai nguyen.
  6. Self-evaluation: Tu danh gia 8 tieu chi theo muc Tot / Kha / Trung binh (Kien thuc, Kha nang hoc hoi, Tinh chu dong, Ky luat, Giao tiep, Teamwork, Giai quyet van de, Dong gop).
  7. Sharing and Feedback: Cam nhan ve chuong trinh, muc do hai long, dong gop cai thien, y kien gioi thieu ban be.

## 5. Thang diem danh gia cuoi ky (Tong 10 diem)
- Chuyen can: 1.0 diem (0.1 diem / buoi)
- Thai do: 1.0 diem
- Tac phong: 1.0 diem
- Tham du Event: 1.0 diem
- Du an (Project): 6.0 diem
- Yeu cau tong ket: Toi thieu 7.0 / 10 diem de duoc cap chung nhan hoan thanh chuong trinh.

Chi tiet thang diem Project (6.0 diem):
- Y tuong va muc tieu: 1.0 diem (Boi canh thuc te, muc tieu cu the, phu hop voi chuong trinh AWS).
- Kien truc va thiet ke ky thuat: 2.0 diem (So do ro rang, ly do chon toi thieu 3 dich vu AWS, bao mat IAM Least Privilege, kha nang mo rong).
- Trien khai va lab step-by-step: 2.0 diem (Prerequisite, huong dan tung buoc co screenshot/lenh CLI, test & validation, clean-up ro rang).
- Tai lieu workshop va trinh bay: 0.5 diem (Song ngu tieng Anh / tieng Viet, dung layout Hugo template, khong loi chinh ta).
- Dong gop ca nhan: 0.5 diem (Co tuy bien sang tao, khong copy y nguyen, reflection ve kho khan va bai hoc).

## 6. Xu ly vi pham
- Di tre hon 45 phut (tinh nhu vang mat): Tru 0.1 diem / lan.
- Khong tham du event bat buoc: Tru 0.5 diem / lan.
- Vi pham quy dinh trang phuc: Tru 0.5 diem / lan.
- Vi pham quy dinh di chuyen / an uong: Tru 0.5 diem / lan.
